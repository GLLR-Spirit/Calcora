Formula reference

  • Ohm's law: V = R × I
  • Electrical power: P = V × I
  • Ideal voltage divider: Vout = Vin × R₂ / (R₁ + R₂)

Important information

These formulas assume simple DC circuits and ideal components. Check component ratings, circuit topology and units before applying them.
