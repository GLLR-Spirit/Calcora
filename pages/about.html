Tools that make learning and everyday calculations clearer.

Calcora brings practical calculators, educational explanations and study-planning tools together in one accessible place.

For school and university

Calculate averages, explore subject resources and organize revision with tools designed to support learning.

For practical calculations

Use mathematics, science, conversion and finance calculators for common questions and everyday tasks.

Local-first study tools

Many student tools save information in your browser instead of requiring an account. Check each tool for its own storage and backup details.

A learning aid, not an authority

Academic rules vary between institutions and years. Review displayed formulas and confirm important results with the relevant institution.

Our approach

Calcora aims to be useful, understandable and lightweight. The project is developed iteratively: existing tools are reviewed, tested and improved before new features are added.
