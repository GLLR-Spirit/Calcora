Frequently asked questions

Learn how Calcora works, how your data is handled and what to check before relying on an academic result.

Is Calcora free?

Yes. Calcora's static calculators and local student tools are free to use.

Does Calcora need an account or internet connection?

Most calculators and saved-in-browser study tools work without an account. You need a connection to open the hosted site initially, and online features may behave differently.

Where is my study data stored?

Student workspace, flashcards and focus-session progress are stored in your browser on this device. Clearing browser data can erase them.

Can I move my data to another device?

Use the export or backup option available in the relevant tool before changing devices. Import only backup files you trust.

Are grade and university calculations official?

No. Rules can differ by school, university, program and academic year. Review each tool's formula and confirm it with your institution before making an important decision.

Which languages are supported?

The main interface supports English, French and Arabic. Some individual content pages may contain text that has not been translated completely yet.

How can I track revision and focus time?

Use the revision studio to create flashcards and practice quizzes, the study planner to organize sessions, the exam planner for countdowns, and the focus session timer for timed study and breaks.

What should I do if a calculator gives an unexpected result?

Check the entered values, decimal separators, coefficients, units and displayed formula. If the issue persists, use the contact page and include the tool name and steps to reproduce it. Do not send private student records.

Important note

Calcora is an educational aid, not an official source for institutional decisions. Verify important results against the current rules that apply to you.
