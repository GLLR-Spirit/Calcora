# Algerian grading calculator: accuracy policy

Calcora currently provides generic mathematical calculators with user-editable values. It does **not** claim that one set of coefficients or formulas is valid for every Algerian school, BAC stream, university, field, institution or academic year.

## Implemented generic formulas
- Weighted average: sum(mark × coefficient) / sum(coefficients).
- Simple average: sum(marks) / number of marks.
- Two-period annual average: weighted mean of two semester averages, with editable weights.
- Required remaining mark: (target average × total coefficient − existing weighted total) / remaining coefficient.

## Before publishing official profiles
For each profile, record the education level, stream/field, institution if relevant, academic year, exact official source, publication/review dates, mark scale, subject/module coefficients and special calculation rules. Verify sources with the Algerian Ministry of National Education, Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, or the institution's official regulations. Keep unverified coefficients editable; never infer them from another stream or university.
