Formula reference

  • Average speed: v = distance / time
  • Acceleration: a = (final velocity − initial velocity) / time
  • Newton's second law: F = m × a

Important information

These formulas assume the usual introductory models. Keep units consistent and check whether a problem uses average or instantaneous quantities.
