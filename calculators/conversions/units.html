Choose a source and target unit, then convert the value using their metric base-unit factors.

Understand metric unit conversion

Formula: converted value = amount × source-unit factor ÷ target-unit factor.

Worked example: 2.5 km to m = 2.5 × 1,000 = 2,500 m.

Steps: choose units from the same dimension; convert through the metric base unit; read the result in the target unit.

Length, mass and volume are different dimensions. A length unit cannot be converted into a mass or volume unit.
