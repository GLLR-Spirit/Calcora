Finance calculators

Estimate interest and loan repayments with transparent formulas. Results are estimates, not financial advice or lender offers.

Before you use these estimates

The calculators do not include taxes, fees, insurance, variable rates or lender-specific rules. Confirm all terms with the relevant institution. No live currency conversion is provided.

Simple interest

Calculate interest that is applied only to the original principal.

Formula: I = P × r × t ÷ 100

Compound interest

Calculate growth when interest is added to the balance at regular intervals.

Formula: A = P × (1 + r ÷ n)^(n × t)

Loan repayment estimate

Estimate a fixed monthly payment for a loan with a constant annual rate.

Formula: M = P × i ÷ (1 − (1 + i)^−n)
