Percentage calculator

Learn how the percentage is calculated

Formula: part = value × percentage ÷ 100.

Worked example: 15% of 200 = 200 × 15 ÷ 100 = 30.

Steps: enter the original value; enter the percentage; multiply them and divide by 100.

The result is the part of the original value. For a 15% increase, add 30 to 200 to get 230.
